## 📖 Overview
**Master of the Air** is a World War II drama series that follows the courageous men of the 100th Bomb Group of the U.S. Army Air Forces. It showcases their bravery, brotherhood, and struggles as they carry out dangerous missions over Nazi Germany.

## 🛩️ Main Cast
- Austin Butler as Major Gale Cleven  
- Callum Turner as Major John Egan  
- Anthony Boyle as Lt. Harry Crosby  
- Nate Mann as Lt. Robert “Rosie” Rosenthal  
- Barry Keoghan as Lt. Curtis Biddick  

## 🌍 Setting
The story takes place during **World War II** in Europe, primarily focusing on the aerial battles fought by American bomber crews.

## 💭 Why I Like It
> “Master of the Air” captures both the intensity of war and the deep bond between soldiers.  
> The visuals, emotional storytelling, and historical accuracy make it a powerful and inspiring series.

### 🎖️ Quick Facts
- **Genre:** War, Drama, History  
- **Created by:** John Orloff  
- **Produced by:** Steven Spielberg & Tom Hanks  
- **Streaming on:** Apple TV+
